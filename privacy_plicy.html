Privacy Policy

Thank you for using the NipBazar app. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our platform to connect with local grocery stores.

Information Collection

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, delivery address, and pincode, which is necessary for processing your orders.

Use of Information

The information we collect is used solely to facilitate your orders from local shops, ensure the functionality of the app, and communicate with you regarding your account or orders.

Data Security and Third Parties

We implement strict security measures to keep your data safe. Your information is shared with verified local shopkeepers or our local delivery hub only as necessary to fulfill your order delivery.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of the personal data we have collected about you at any time.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding our Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@nipbazar.in.
